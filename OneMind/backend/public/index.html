Frequently asked questions

OneMind is a desktop AI workspace that brings chat, writing, knowledge management, and agent workflows into one native app. Instead of switching between browser tabs and AI tools, you get a single workspace that runs alongside your other apps.
OneMind supports OpenAI (GPT-4o, GPT-4), Anthropic (Claude), Google (Gemini), Meta (Llama via Ollama), OpenRouter, and any OpenAI-compatible API. You bring your own keys and switch models freely.
Yes. OneMind is local-first. Your conversations, documents, and knowledge base are stored on your device in a local SQLite database. API keys are stored in your OS secure keychain. Nothing is sent to our servers.
OneMind is available for macOS and Windows. It's built with Tauri 2, giving you a truly native desktop experience with a small footprint.
OneMind is currently in early access. We're working on pricing — early adopters will get special rates. The app uses your own API keys, so you only pay for what you use with AI providers.
ChatGPT and Claude are single-model web apps. OneMind is a desktop workspace that combines multiple models, organized workspaces (chat, writing, knowledge), agent capabilities, and a command bar you can invoke from any app — no browser tab needed.
